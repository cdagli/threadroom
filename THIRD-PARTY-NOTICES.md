## EMS single-line satin lettering

`lib/fonts-satin/readability.json`, `delight.json`, `pancakes.json`, `tech.json`,
`herculean.json`, `osmotron.json` and `allure.json` are generated by
`scripts/generate-satin-fonts.py` from the EMS single-line SVG fonts vendored unchanged
in `scripts/ems/`, downloaded from https://gitlab.com/oskay/svg-fonts (`fonts/EMS/`).
The generator verifies each file's SHA-256, scales it to capital height, merges dots
drawn as small shapes into one stroke and simplifies polylines (0.004 em).

The EMS fonts were created by Sheldon B. Michaels, with SVG font conversion by
Windell H. Oskay, as derivatives of fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The
licence text is `scripts/ems/OFL.txt`; each SVG's `<metadata>` names its origin:

Regenerate offline with `.venv/bin/python scripts/generate-satin-fonts.py`; add `--check` to verify byte-identical JSON.
The generator verifies the source checksum and every Hershey record length.
The data retains advances and strokes in em units (capital height = 1).
Turkish forms compose the existing letters with Hershey dots, commas and the
lower half of the Hershey `o` for the breve; dotless `ı` removes the detached dot
from `i`. Curly quotes/dashes reuse the corresponding Hershey ASCII marks.
